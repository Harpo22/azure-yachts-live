Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Who We Are

Azure Yachts S.A.M. ("Azure", "we", "us") is a private yacht charter company registered in Monaco. Our registered address is 7 Quai Antoine 1er, Monte Carlo, Monaco 98000. We process personal data in accordance with applicable privacy laws including GDPR where relevant.

2. Data We Collect

When you submit a charter enquiry, we collect:

  • Name, email address and telephone number
  • Nationality and referral source
  • Charter preferences (vessel, destination, dates, budget)
  • Any additional information you choose to share in your enquiry

We also collect standard web analytics data (page views, device type, approximate location) to improve our website.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your charter enquiry and provide quotes
  • To send occasional updates about our fleet and destinations (only with your consent)
  • To improve our services and website
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data with trusted service providers (such as FormSubmit for email delivery) who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Retention

Enquiry data is retained for up to 3 years. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at privacy@azureyachts.com.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. You may also withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please contact privacy@azureyachts.com.

7. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies only to ensure it functions correctly. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Contact

For privacy-related queries, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@azureyachts.com or write to us at 7 Quai Antoine 1er, Monte Carlo, Monaco 98000.
