The Azure Experience

Every Detail,
Considered

A charter with Azure is more than a voyage. It is a seamless orchestration of the world's finest elements, delivered with an invisible precision that allows you to simply be present.

01

Private Culinary Arts

Your dedicated private chef, trained at the world's most lauded establishments, curates menus around your palate. The freshest produce sourced at every port. Wine pairings selected by our Master Sommelier.

02

Concierge Without Limits

Secure a table at a fully booked restaurant ashore. Arrange a private helicopter excursion over volcanic landscapes. Our concierge operates on the principle that "impossible" is simply a matter of timing.

03

Wellness & Restoration

Aboard every Azure vessel, a fully equipped spa awaits. Your personal therapist, yoga instructor, and nutritionist travel with the yacht. The sea, it turns out, is the finest place to restore body and mind.

04

Water Toy Fleet

Each yacht carries a full complement of water toys: jet skis, seabobs, dive equipment, paddleboards, and a custom tender. Adventure at sea is always available, as quietly or exuberantly as you wish.
